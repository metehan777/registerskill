## Your Infrastructure. Your Data. Your Rules.

Choose where your workspace runs and which models your agents use.

### Self-hosted by default

Run Tale on your own infrastructure or choose managed hosting. Local models keep inference local; external providers, connectors, and tools follow the connections you configure.

### Security built in

Manage members, roles, single sign-on, budgets, and configured guardrails in the same product. Use audit records and run history to investigate what happened.

### Fully open source

The entire codebase is public under the MIT license. Read it, audit it, change what you need - without asking anyone.

## Choose the runtime for each agent
