## Common questions about AI drawing reflection

### What is InnerCanvas?

InnerCanvas at innercanvas.app is a standalone web tool for adults who want structured, private drawing-based self-reflection. It provides bounded observations and reflection prompts; it is not affiliated with similarly named art-therapy, counseling, or e-commerce services.

### Is InnerCanvas a diagnosis or psychological assessment?

No. InnerCanvas is for adult self-reflection and education. It does not diagnose, screen, treat, or verify mental health conditions, personality traits, trauma, intelligence, or protected traits.

### Who is InnerCanvas for?

InnerCanvas is for adults 18 and older who want a private, structured way to reflect on a drawing. It is not for evaluating children, employees, students, patients, or another person's health or safety.

### What happens to my drawing?

A draft stays in your browser until you submit it. Submitted drawings, previews, and reports remain private in your account until you delete the content or account.

### Can I upload a paper drawing?

Yes. You can draw in the browser or upload one clear JPG, PNG, or HEIC image for the selected test.

### What does a complete report include?

A complete report separates visible observations from cautious possible reflections, includes questions for personal exploration, and shows evidence limits, conflicting findings, and sources where applicable.

### What happens after I submit a drawing?

After submission, the drawing and minimum analysis context are sent to the configured model provider to generate the report. Submitted drawings, previews, and reports remain in your private account until you delete them, except for legally required transaction records.

### Does InnerCanvas use drawings to train AI models?

No. Customer drawings are not used to train InnerCanvas models unless you give a separate, explicit, revocable opt-in. Optional analytics is also consent-based and excludes drawing content and report text.
