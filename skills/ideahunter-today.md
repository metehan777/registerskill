## Before you pick an idea

### What makes an IdeaHunter idea validated?

A validated idea is tied to observable demand evidence such as repeated user complaints, buyer requests, app reviews, job posts, open-source activity, or growth signals. It is a research lead, not a guarantee of product-market fit, so every idea also includes a next validation step.

### Are these ideas realistic for one person to build?

IdeaHunter exposes estimated MVP weeks, required team size, technical complexity, and execution feasibility. The homepage prioritizes narrow opportunities that a solo founder can test or ship in roughly one to two weeks.

### Is IdeaHunter only for developers?

No. It is designed for indie hackers, solo entrepreneurs, and people building a side business with AI coding tools, no-code tools, or traditional development. The important constraint is the ability to test a small product quickly.

### How is this different from a list of AI-generated startup ideas?

The starting point is external market evidence, not a blank prompt. You can inspect the source, buyer pain, product gap, build scope, revenue model, and related growth examples before deciding whether to build.
